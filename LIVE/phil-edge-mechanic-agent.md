## 2015 Ford Edge 2.0L EcoBoost - Critical Information

### Engine Specs
- Displacement: 2.0L
- Horsepower: 245 hp @ 5,500 rpm
- Torque: 275 lb-ft @ 3,000 rpm
- Turbocharger: Single turbo with intercooler
- Fuel System: Direct Injection (GDI) - no fuel washing intake valves
- Oil Capacity: 5.8 quarts with filter

### Most Common Problems

**1. Carbon Buildup on Intake Valves (GDI Issue)**
- **Symptom**: Rough idle, hesitation, reduced power, misfires
- **Cause**: GDI engines don't have fuel washing valves like traditional engines
- **Solution**: CRC GDI IVD Intake Valve Cleaner treatment every 10,000 miles (~$40-60/treatment at shop, DIY ~$15)
- **Major Service**: Full intake valve cleaning (remove intake manifold) - 4-6 hours labor

**2. Exhaust Manifold Cracks**
- **Symptom**: Ticking/chirping noise, poor performance, excessive boost, exhaust smell inside cabin
- **Cause**: Heat cycling and manufacturing defect in early 2015 models
- **TSB**: Ford issued multiple TSBs for this issue
- **Solution**: Replace exhaust manifold
- **Cost**: $150-280 parts + 2-3 hours labor = $400-700 independent, $500-850 dealership

**3. Coolant in Cylinders (TSB 19-2346)**
- **Symptom**: Hard to start, low coolant level, #2 spark plug difficult to remove, rough running
- **Applies to**: 2015-2018 Edge, 2017-2019 Escape/Fusion
- **Cause**: Cylinder head gasket or intake manifold gasket leak
- **Solution**: Replace gasket(s), may need cylinder head resurfacing
- **Cost**: $800-1,500 labor + $200-400 parts

**4. Turbo/Boost Control Solenoid Failure**
- **Symptom**: Low boost under acceleration, engine code P0299, poor performance, reduced fuel economy
- **Cause**: Carbon deposits or electrical failure
- **Solution**: Replace boost control solenoid
- **Cost**: $180-280 parts + 1-2 hours labor = $300-500 independent, $400-600 dealership

**5. Low-Pressure Fuel Pump Failure**
- **Symptom**: Rough idle, poor acceleration, engine light, misfires, won't start
- **Solution**: Replace fuel pump module in tank
- **Cost**: $400-600 parts + 3-4 hours labor = $850-1,200 independent, $1,000-1,400 dealership

**6. Intake Valve and Turbo Carbon Cleaning (Preventive)**
- **Frequency**: Every 10,000 miles for GDI engines
- **Cost**: $40-60 fuel additive treatment or $400-600 full service with intake manifold removal
